Fazker (فَذَكِّرْ)

Privacy Policy · سياسة الخصوصية

Last updated · آخر تحديث: October 5, 2026
English العربية

Fazker is an Islamic utility app that provides Quran reading and audio, Azkar, prayer times, Qibla direction, Tasbeeh, reminders, and related features. We respect your privacy and aim to collect only the data necessary to provide these features.

Information We May Use

Location

The app may request access to your location to calculate prayer times and Qibla direction. Where possible, this data is processed on-device. If location is not available, the app may use a manually selected city or cached location.

Notifications

The app may use local notifications for prayer reminders, Adhan alerts, and Dhikr reminders that you choose to enable.

Microphone

If voice input is enabled for the Assistant, microphone access is used only when you actively choose to speak a command.

App Preferences

Settings such as selected reciter, reminder preferences, Tasbeeh settings, and similar preferences may be stored locally on your device.

How Data Is Handled

Third-Party Services

Fazker may use third-party services for features such as crash reporting, analytics, content delivery, audio delivery, or notifications. These services may process limited technical data according to their own privacy policies. The exact services used may change as the app evolves.

Quran and Audio Content

The app may retrieve Quran audio, recitation metadata, and other content from external providers or content delivery networks. Requests to these services may include ordinary network information such as IP address and device/network metadata that is normally transmitted when accessing online content.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your personal data with advertisers.

Data Retention

Most settings and cached content are stored locally on your device. Cached audio and temporary files may be removed by the operating system or by the app. If analytics or crash-reporting services are enabled, their retention is governed by the relevant service provider.

Children

Fazker is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is required to provide app functionality.

Your Choices

You can control permissions such as Location, Microphone, and Notifications from your device settings. You can also disable prayer, Adhan, and Dhikr reminders from within the app.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when features or third-party services change. The updated date at the top of this page will reflect the latest revision.

Contact
For privacy questions or requests, contact us at: iosayed1@gmail.com
